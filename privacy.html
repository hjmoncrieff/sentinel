Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

SENTINEL is a research dashboard. You can use all of it without an account, and by default it sets no cookies and does not track you.

Browsing the dashboard

The public dashboard is a static website. It does not use cookies, accounts, advertising, or tracking pixels, and it does not store anything in your browser unless you respond to the analytics prompt described below.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may process your IP address and request details to deliver and secure the site. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Third-party resources

To draw maps, charts, and typography, your browser loads files directly from these providers. Each receives your IP address and browser details when it serves a file:

  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com): typefaces.
  • jsDelivr (cdn.jsdelivr.net): D3, TopoJSON, and world map outlines.
  • unpkg (unpkg.com): the Leaflet map library.
  • cdnjs / Cloudflare (cdnjs.cloudflare.com): Chart.js.

Links to news sources and datasets take you to other websites, which have their own policies.

Weekly brief and feedback forms

If you subscribe to the weekly brief or send feedback, the information you type goes to Formspree, our form processor, and is forwarded to the project maintainers. For the brief, that is your email address. For feedback, it is the category, country, your message, and, if you add it, your email address.

  • We use it only to send the brief or to respond to your feedback.
  • We never sell or share it for marketing.
  • You can ask us to delete it, or to remove you from the brief, at any time. See Contact.

Optional analytics

The site can count anonymous page views with GoatCounter, a privacy-focused service that uses no cookies and builds no personal profiles. It is off unless you choose Allow in the consent prompt. Your choice is saved in your browser's local storage under sentinel-analytics-consent, and you can change it here:

Analyst console

The analyst console is a separate, invitation-only tool for credentialed researchers. It uses Supabase for sign-in and browser storage to keep sessions. Its data is not part of the public dashboard.

Children

SENTINEL is intended for researchers, students, and professionals. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We update this page when the site's data practices change. The date at the top shows the latest revision.

Contact

For privacy requests or questions, use the feedback form on the dashboard or open an issue on GitHub. Please don't post personal data in a public issue. Use the feedback form for anything private.
