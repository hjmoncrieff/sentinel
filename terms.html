Research and informational use

SENTINEL monitors civil-military relations, political stability, and security events across Latin America and the Caribbean. It is provided for research, education, journalism, and general information. It is not operational, security, legal, investment, or travel advice, and it should not be your only basis for decisions that affect people's safety.

How the data is made, and its limits

  • Events are compiled from open-source reporting and structured datasets. Many are classified, summarized, and interpreted with AI assistance inside a supervised research workflow.
  • Automated coding can misread a report. It can assign the wrong type or location, merge distinct incidents, or miss events altogether.
  • Coverage depends on what sources report and publish, and it varies by country and period.
  • Each event records its sources and review status. Check the original reporting before relying on any single record.
